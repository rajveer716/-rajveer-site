About

From the field to the frontier.

The short version of how a farm kid became the person his class benchmarks against.

I grew up around farmland near Indore, in a family that has worked that land for generations. That's still home — and it's also the reason I think in systems: soil, seasons, yield, risk. Somewhere along the way I pointed the same instincts at AI, and it stuck.

Now I'm a second-year BBA Marketing student at AIMSR, and my projects have ended up being the ones other students in Section F measure theirs against. I like it that way. It means I don't get to coast.

Long-term, the two halves come back together: I want to bring what I'm learning about AI and marketing back to modernize how my family's farm operates.
